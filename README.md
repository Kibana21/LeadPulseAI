## 🧭 Executive Summary

**Smart Voice-Enabled Generative AI for Appointment Setting and Life-Cycle Engagement**

The *Voice Generated Agent Assistant* leverages **Voice-Enabled Generative AI** to automate and enhance customer outreach. By combining natural voice interaction, calendar synchronization, and personalized engagement, the system supports AIA agents in scheduling appointments, delivering campaign messages, and handling life-cycle follow-ups such as birthdays and policy anniversaries — all through intelligent voice automation.

This initiative aligns with AIA’s strategic focus on **innovation, personalization, and scalable customer engagement**, empowering agents to focus on higher-value interactions while ensuring consistent and compliant client communication.

## ⚠️ Problem Statements

### 1. Manual Repetitive Tasks

Agents spend excessive time manually scheduling appointments and following up on client interactions. This reduces productivity and increases response times.

### 2. Missed Opportunities

Inefficient outreach processes often lead to missed sales or engagement opportunities, directly affecting business growth.

### 3. Lack of Automation

The absence of intelligent support tools limits the agents’ ability to provide timely, personalized, and compliant communication.

## ⚙️ Key Features

| Feature                                    | Function                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Natural Language Understanding (NLU)**   | Understands customer intent, tone, and context in real-time conversations.                        |
| **Calendar Integration**                   | Syncs with agent and customer calendars for real-time scheduling.                                 |
| **Life-Cycle Trigger Engine**              | Detects upcoming events (birthdays, renewals, anniversaries) and initiates personalized outreach. |
| **Conversation Intelligence**              | Captures insights from past interactions to tailor future conversations.                          |
| **Content Generation & Summarization**     | Generates personalized call scripts, follow-up summaries, and notes for agents.                   |
| **Voice Synthesis & Multilingual Support** | Converts AI responses into natural, human-like voice across multiple languages.                   |
| **Compliance & Governance**                | Ensures data protection and adherence to PDPA / PDPD guidelines.                                  |
| **CRM & API Integration**                  | Connects with tools such as Salesforce, iSmart, or Plan360 for customer data synchronization.     |

## 🧱 Technology Stack Summary
