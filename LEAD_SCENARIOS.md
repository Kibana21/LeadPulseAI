## 🛡️ Ethical Trigger Detection Framework

**All lead triggers are detected through LEGITIMATE, CONSENT-BASED sources:**

1. ✅ **Customer Self-Reporting** - Forms, surveys, profile updates
2. ✅ **Website Behavior** - Calculator usage, guide downloads, page visits
3. ✅ **CRM Events** - Policy anniversaries, birthdays, renewals
4. ✅ **Opt-in Communications** - Newsletter responses, SMS replies
5. ✅ **Public Records** - Marriage certificates, property transactions (with consent)
6. ✅ **Partner Integrations** - Hospital partnerships (maternity packages), property portals

**❌ NEVER:** Social media stalking, unauthorized data scraping, privacy invasion
