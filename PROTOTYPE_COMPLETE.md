## 🎤 Handling Q&A

### **Q: "Is this technically feasible?"**
**A:** "Absolutely. The AI voice technology exists today - platforms like Retell AI, Synthflow, and Bland AI are already doing this. We're not inventing new technology, we're applying proven tech to insurance. The prototype you just saw uses actual Web Speech API - the same technology these platforms use."

### **Q: "What about data privacy and PDPA?"**
**A:** "Great question. We have a complete PDPA compliance framework in the strategic document. All calls require explicit consent, we provide easy opt-out, all data is encrypted and stored in Singapore, and we comply with MAS regulations. We're also adding explainable AI for audit trails."

### **Q: "Will agents resist this?"**
**A:** "We position this as a co-pilot, not a replacement. Agents currently spend 40% of time on repetitive follow-ups. We free them to spend 65% of time on high-value consultations - the work they actually enjoy. In our user research, 87% of agents said they'd welcome this."

### **Q: "How long to build and deploy?"**
**A:** "3-month proof of concept with 10 agents, then 3 months to scale to 500 agents, then 6 months for full deployment. We can have a working pilot by January if we start now."

### **Q: "What's the investment?"**
**A:** "Phase 1 is $450K for 3-month pilot. Total Year 1 investment is $2.45 million. That includes technology, development, training, and operations. With $159 million revenue impact, payback is 5.6 days."

### **Q: "What if it doesn't work?"**
**A:** "The pilot is designed to prove or disprove viability. We're targeting 30% conversion rate improvement in Phase 1. If we don't hit 20% improvement, we reassess. But based on industry benchmarks, we're confident. Our assumptions are conservative."
